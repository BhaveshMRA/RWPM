\section{Limitations and Next Steps}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Labels.} Whether two skills are derived is a judgement. The boundary between a
derived variant and a template sibling is least clear for a series of skills written by one
author from a personal template. We reduce this risk with a written rubric, independent double
labelling, an agreement measure, and discussion of every disagreement, but the gold set still
reflects our reading of the rubric.

\paragraph{Sampling and features.} The labelled pairs are stratified by similarity, so class
proportions in the gold set do not reflect proportions in the population; we therefore also
report results per band. The boilerplate threshold is a heuristic, and a widely copied skill
that is often renamed can push its own lines over it. The same-owner feature may let the model
learn authors' habits rather than derivation; the ablation shows how much it contributes.

\paragraph{Baseline.} Our SkillReuse baseline is re-implemented from the paper and trained on
our gold set. Its numbers are not comparable to those reported by Zhu et
al.~\cite{zhu2026skillreuse}.

\paragraph{Data.} GitSkills covers default branches of public repositories only and is
itself a lower bound on the population~\cite{destefanis2027gitskills}. Because the official
sample draws distinct contents uniformly, families are truncated, so RQ3 underestimates
family-level reuse. Commit history is available for only \ResWorkHistory{} of the
\ResWorkContents{} skills in our working set, and \texttt{first\_commit\_at} dates the file at
its current path, so a renamed or moved skill looks newer than it is~\cite{gitskills_sample}.

\paragraph{Phase 3.} We will (i) run the pipeline on the full GitSkills dataset; (ii) answer
RQ4 by retrieving, for the largest families, the history of the earliest member through the
GitHub API, hashing each past version, and checking whether copies match the current version,
an older version, or neither, which separates consistent from inconsistent change
propagation~\cite{kim2005genealogy}; (iii) fetch commit dates for pairs the dataset cannot
orient; (iv) add multilingual sentence embeddings to recover reworded and translated copies;
and (v) compare against SkillReuse's released implementation and on SkillReuse-Bench.
